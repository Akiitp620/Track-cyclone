# CycloneShield AI maintains a digital representation of infrastructure assets and recomputes their risk state as cyclone scenarios change.

### AI-Powered Cyclone Impact & Infrastructure Vulnerability Digital Twin

> **From cyclone scenarios to infrastructure-level risk and pre-landfall action plans.**

CycloneShield AI is a decision-support platform that helps disaster-management teams understand **which critical infrastructure assets are most vulnerable under a simulated cyclone scenario** and what actions should be prioritized before landfall.

Instead of acting as another weather dashboard or cyclone prediction system, CycloneShield AI combines **scenario simulation, deterministic risk analysis, geospatial evidence, infrastructure data, and AI-assisted decision support** into a single workflow.

---

## The Problem

Cyclones do not impact every location or infrastructure asset equally.

A cyclone scenario may involve:

- Extreme wind
- Heavy rainfall
- Coastal exposure
- Changing cyclone tracks
- High population concentration
- Vulnerable infrastructure
- Limited access routes

However, disaster-management decisions often require a more specific question:

> **"If this cyclone scenario occurs, which infrastructure should we act on first?"**

Traditional weather information provides hazard information, but converting that information into **asset-level operational priorities** requires combining multiple layers of information.

This creates a gap between:

**Hazard Information → Infrastructure Risk → Operational Action**

CycloneShield AI is designed to bridge that gap.

---

# Our Solution

CycloneShield AI creates a digital representation of infrastructure assets and evaluates their risk under different cyclone scenarios.

A user can modify parameters such as:

- Wind speed
- Rainfall
- Hazard radius
- Cyclone track shift

The system then recalculates infrastructure-level risk and identifies:

- Critical assets
- High-risk assets
- Population exposed
- Infrastructure vulnerability
- Geographic concentration of risk
- Recommended pre-landfall actions

### Where AI Fits

CycloneShield separates deterministic risk calculation from AI-assisted reasoning.

The deterministic risk engine calculates infrastructure risk using explicit hazard, exposure, vulnerability, and criticality factors.

AI is then used to interpret these structured risk signals, synthesize available evidence, and generate human-readable pre-landfall decision support.

The AI does not arbitrarily assign infrastructure risk scores — it helps decision-makers understand and act on the calculated results.
